# WheatCoin Community Tokens

Users can create fixed-supply ERC-20 tokens on WheatCoin Mainnet through the
official WheatTokenFactory.

- Creator: <https://explorer.mizodevelopment.com/tokens.html>
- Factory: `0xFaf8588080c78E1679b3eb7E1813eE7D964F88DA`
- Chain ID: `153043`
- Creation fee: `10 WHEAT`
- Decimals: `18`
- Maximum initial supply: `1,000,000,000,000`

The connected wallet pays the creation fee and receives the complete initial
supply. Factory-created tokens have a fixed supply: the template has no mint
function, while holders can transfer or burn their own tokens.

Factory registration confirms that the contract came from the standard
factory. It does not mean Wheat Industries or Mizo Development endorses the
project, creator, price, or safety of a community token. Names and symbols are
not unique, so integrations must identify tokens by contract address.
